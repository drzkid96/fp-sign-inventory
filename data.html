HOW THE DATA IS KEPT

Every layer is rebuilt nightly from its source. Each one downloads as GeoJSON, CSV, KMZ (Google Earth), GeoPackage, shapefile and DXF in CA State Plane Zone 5 (US ft) for CAD. All layers in one GeoPackage, State Plane Zone 5: all-layers-2229.gpkg.

For other tools: the layer list is data/layers.json and the build results are data/layers/index.json; map tiles are PMTiles under data/layers/.
